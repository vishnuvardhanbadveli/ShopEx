One spine. Clear boundaries.
ShopEx turns a natural-language request into a verifiable, human-approved payment state without asking the language model to self-certify.

Model boundary

LLM extracts intent; it does not decide whether payment is allowed.

Human boundary

Checkout cannot proceed until the buyer approves the exact amount.

Provider boundary

Payment and webhook signatures are checked independently on the server.
