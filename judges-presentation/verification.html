08 · payment boundary
Two independent checks protect the boundary.
ShopEx does not trust the client. We verify the payment twice using independent cryptographic paths before marking an order as captured.
01 Client-Side Signature
1
HMAC-SHA256 verification of the payment ID, order ID, and signature.
2
Prevents client-side tampering or spoofed success callbacks.
3
Immediate UI feedback for the buyer agent and user.
02 Server-Side Webhook
1
Raw-body signature verification directly from Razorpay servers.
2
Durable event idempotency to prevent double-processing.
3
Drives the final "Captured" state transition in the database.
RESULT: A durable, append-only audit trail of cryptographic evidence.
